Mama Kids Control by BillionTech

Privacy policy

Effective September 30, 2026

Your screen data stays on the device. Mama Kids Control processes screen information and blocking settings on the device. The app has no parent accounts, ads, analytics or connection to BillionTech servers. The APK includes Internet access for Google Play Billing to check subscription status. Screen content, blocked words, blocked channels, PIN data and recovery answers are not sent to BillionTech. Google processes subscription and payment information under its own privacy notices.

What Mama Kids Control does

Mama Kids Control is a parental control app for Android. A parent installs it on their child's phone. The child can watch any video in the official YouTube app, except: Shorts (if the parent blocks them), channels the parent has blocked, and videos whose title or channel name contains a word on the parent's blocked-words list. Mama Kids Control can also block searching for those words, downloads and app-install buttons, ads and links that leave YouTube, and video-downloader apps on the parent's blocked list.

How Mama Kids Control uses the Accessibility service

To work, Mama Kids Control uses Android's Accessibility service. It is set up to observe only the YouTube app, common web browsers and app stores (only to notice that they opened right after a tap in YouTube), and, if the parent turns this on, the specific apps on the parent's blocked list. It does not observe any other app.

While YouTube is open, Mama Kids Control reads what is shown on screen, such as video titles, channel names, whether the Shorts player is open, the label of a button that was tapped (for example "Download", "Install" or "Search"), and words typed in YouTube's search box. For browsers, app stores and apps on the blocked list, it only uses the fact that the app has opened; it does not read what is shown in them.

It uses this only to decide, on the phone, whether to allow or block what is happening. If something is not allowed, Mama Kids Control pauses playback, leaves the screen or app and shows a friendly "not this one" message. After a video is blocked, it also checks whether YouTube is showing a small picture-in-picture window and keeps that video paused.

This information is processed in the phone's memory and then discarded. It is not saved and it is never sent anywhere. The parent is shown a clear explanation and must agree before the Accessibility service can be turned on.

Information stored on the phone

Mama Kids Control saves only the settings the parent chooses, in the app's private storage on the device:

  • Which rules are turned on (Shorts, blocked words, search, downloads, ads and links, downloader apps).
  • The blocked-words list and the blocked-channels list the parent typed in.
  • The list of blocked apps (their package names), which the parent can edit.
  • The parent PIN, the answers to three security questions the parent chooses, and a PIN recovery code. Each is stored only as a protected, one-way code (a salted hash, further protected by a key kept in the phone's Android Keystore). The PIN, the answers and the recovery code themselves are never stored, never shown again after setup, and never leave the phone. The recovery code is shown once; if the parent chooses "Send to myself", it is shared only to the app the parent picks.
  • Which three security questions were chosen, and counters of wrong attempts (used to pause guessing for a while).
  • Advanced detection settings, if the parent changes them.
  • The date the free trial started and whether a subscription is active.

These settings are excluded from phone backups and are never uploaded.

Subscriptions and payments

Mama Kids Control offers a 7-day free trial, then a subscription (monthly or yearly) sold through Google Play. Payments are processed by Google under the Google Payments Privacy Notice. Mama Kids Control never sees your card or payment details.

To know whether protection should be on, Mama Kids Control checks Google Play Billing for the subscription status and saves the result (active or not) on the device. It also saves the free-trial start date on the device. Google Play processes purchase and subscription information under its own privacy notices. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

Diagnostics

A parent can turn on an optional diagnostics feature for testing. It keeps a copy of the last YouTube screen that Mama Kids Control checked, so the parent can see what the app is able to read. This copy is held only in memory, is replaced with each new check and is erased when the app closes. It is only shared if the parent chooses to copy it themselves.

Children's privacy

Mama Kids Control is meant to be set up and managed by a parent or guardian. It does not create child accounts or profiles, and does not send children's screen information or the family's blocking settings to BillionTech. Subscription checks are handled by Google Play as described above.

Deleting your data

The app's PIN, recovery information, blocking settings, trial date and saved subscription-status result stay in private storage on the phone. They are removed if you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Google Play keeps subscription records according to its own policies; manage or cancel the subscription in Google Play.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds online features, such as a parent dashboard or syncing between devices, this policy will be updated first to explain exactly what is collected and why. The effective date above will change whenever the policy is updated.
