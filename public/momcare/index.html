MamaKidsControl Support

Help for parents using MamaKidsControl on iPhone and iPad.

Contact support

For app issues, subscription questions, or assistance with parental controls, email info@billiontech.ca. Please include your device model, iOS version, and a description of the issue. Never send your parental PIN.

Screen Time permission

Screen Time access is needed for supported app restrictions, schedules, and limits. In MamaKidsControl, select the appropriate device mode and choose Continue. Review Apple's system permission prompt and make your choice. If you decline, protection features that depend on Screen Time will not be available.

App restrictions and parental PIN

Use the app's controls to choose supported apps, categories, and websites to restrict. The parent PIN protects settings within MamaKidsControl; it does not replace your device passcode or control Apple's Settings app.

Subscriptions and purchases

Premium includes custom schedules and daily usage limits. Subscription prices and available trial terms appear in the app before purchase.

  • To manage or cancel: open iPhone or iPad Settings → your name → Subscriptions and select MamaKidsControl.
  • To restore a previous purchase: open MamaKidsControl's subscription screen and select Restore Purchases, using the Apple Account that made the purchase.
