Study Alberta driving knowledge on your phone

The Alberta Class 7 & 4 app gives learners a convenient way to practice driving knowledge questions while preparing for Alberta licensing exams.

Practice areas

  • Alberta Class 7 learner knowledge-test practice.
  • Alberta Class 4 professional licence knowledge-test practice.
  • Repeatable quiz sessions for study and review.
  • Mobile access on iPhone, iPad and Android.

Who it is for

It is designed for Alberta learners and drivers who want an additional practice tool before taking a knowledge test.

Important: This is an independent educational practice app from BILLION TECH INC. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Government of Alberta. Official Alberta driver guides and registry information remain the authoritative sources.
