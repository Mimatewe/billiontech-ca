Privacy Policy — Alberta Class 7 & 4

Effective: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BILLION TECH INC. handles information in the Alberta Class 7 & 4 educational practice app.

Information and app use

The app is designed for driving-knowledge practice. Quiz progress and similar study information may be stored locally on the user's device. BILLION TECH INC. does not require an account to use the core practice features.

Advertising and third-party services

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertising. Google and its advertising technologies may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and related technical data as permitted by the user's device settings, consent choices, and applicable law. This processing is performed by Google under its own privacy terms.

How information is used

Information processed through third-party services may be used to provide and measure advertising, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain service reliability, and understand technical performance.

Sharing

BILLION TECH INC. does not sell personal information. Data processed by third-party advertising or platform services may be transmitted to those providers as necessary to provide their services.

Security, retention and deletion

Local study data remains on the device unless removed by the user, clearing app data, or uninstalling the app. Data processed independently by third-party providers is subject to their retention and security practices. BILLION TECH INC. uses reasonable safeguards for information under its control.

Children

This app is an educational driving-test practice tool and is not designed as a child-directed service.

Your choices

Users can manage advertising and privacy choices through applicable device settings and any consent controls presented in the app.

Independent educational app

Alberta Class 7 & 4 is an independent educational practice app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Government of Alberta.

Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to BILLION TECH INC. through the contact information at billiontech.ca.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when appropriate.
